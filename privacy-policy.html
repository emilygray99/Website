Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026

This policy is written in plain English so it's actually readable. It isn't a substitute for formal legal advice, if you have specific concerns about your obligations, please have a solicitor review this document alongside the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) guidance.

Who we are

Fitness By Em ("we", "us", "our") is the online coaching business operated by Emily Gray, providing personalised fitness and nutrition coaching for women across Australia. You can contact us at admin@fitnessbyem.com.au with any privacy questions.

What personal information we collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your name, email address and phone number, when you submit an application or enquiry form.
  • Health and lifestyle information: details you choose to share in our coaching application, such as injuries, health conditions, dietary preferences, activity level and fitness goals.
  • Communications: any messages you send us by email, form, or social media.
  • Usage data: general website analytics such as pages visited and device type, collected via tools like Google Analytics and Meta Pixel where enabled.

How we collect it

Information is collected directly from you when you complete our application form, book a consultation, contact us by email, or interact with our social media pages. Our website is hosted on Netlify, and form submissions are processed through Netlify's form handling service.

Why we collect and use it

We use your personal information to:

  • Assess whether our coaching programs are a good fit for you, and build a program around your goals, health history and preferences.
  • Contact you about your application, bookings, and ongoing coaching.
  • Improve our website and services based on general usage trends.
  • With your consent, send you marketing communications about our programs, guides and offers. You can opt out at any time.

Health information

Because coaching applications may include health-related information, we treat this data with particular care. Under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), businesses that collect health information are required to comply with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) regardless of business size, the usual small business exemption does not apply to health service providers. We only use your health information to design and deliver your coaching program, and we do not sell or disclose it for unrelated purposes.

Who we share information with

We don't sell your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers who help us run our business, including:

  • Netlify, our website host and form processor.
  • Calendly, for booking consultation calls.
  • Newie, the platform used to deliver and manage active coaching programs.
  • Google Analytics and Meta (Facebook/Instagram), for website analytics and advertising, where these tools are active on our site.

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing how they handle data on our behalf.

Data storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we hold from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access. Information submitted through our website is stored securely with our service providers listed above. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Access and correction

You can ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing admin@fitnessbyem.com.au. We'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Cookies and tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies through tools like Google Analytics and Meta Pixel to understand how visitors use our site and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we've handled your personal information, please contact us first at admin@fitnessbyem.com.au so we can try to resolve it directly. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
