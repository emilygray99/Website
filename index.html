Meet your coach

Hi, I'm Emily.

I'm a Masters Graduate Exercise Physiologist and Myotherapist, which means my approach to weight loss is grounded in how bodies actually work, not what's trending.

I specialise in helping women lose weight sustainably. Not the kind where you white-knuckle a 1200-calorie diet for 8 weeks and gain it all back. The kind where you lose it, keep it off, and actually enjoy the process, without cutting out the foods you love.

I've helped over 450 women do exactly that, and the difference every time is having a plan built for your body, your life, and your goals.
