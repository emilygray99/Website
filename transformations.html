Common questions

Frequently asked

Are these weight loss results typical?+

Results vary depending on starting point, program adherence, and individual metabolism. The clients shown lost 7-12kg over 15-20 weeks, which is consistent with a sustainable rate of 0.25-0.75kg per week. Emily doesn't promise specific outcomes, she builds personalised plans designed to give each woman the best possible results for her specific body and lifestyle.

How long does it take to see results with Fitness By Em?+

Most clients notice changes in energy, strength, and body composition within 4-6 weeks. Visible weight loss results are typically significant by weeks 8-12. The minimum coaching commitment is 12 weeks because this is the period required for meaningful, lasting fat loss, not a quick fix that reverses.

Do clients keep the weight off after coaching ends?+

Sustainable results are the foundation of every Fitness By Em program. Rather than extreme protocols that require ongoing restriction, Emily builds habits and nutritional understanding that clients maintain independently. Many clients continue losing weight or maintaining their results long after their 12-week program concludes.
