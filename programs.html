Common questions

Frequently asked

What is included in the Signature Method?+

The Signature Method includes a fully personalised training program, a personalised meal plan, weekly 1:1 check-ins, daily messaging support, video form checks, and access to the private community group. It's the recommended option for women focused on sustainable weight loss.

How long is the minimum coaching commitment?+

All 1:1 coaching programs require a 12-week minimum commitment. This is the minimum time needed to build genuine habits and see meaningful lasting weight loss results.

Can I train at home without a gym membership?+

Yes. Both the Essential Plan and Signature Method are available for home or gym training. Your program is designed around what equipment and environment you have access to.

What makes Fitness By Em different from other online coaches?+

Emily Gray holds a Masters Graduate in Exercise Physiology, a postgraduate clinical qualification. Her programs are built on evidence-based exercise science and individualised nutrition, not generic templates or influencer trends.

How much weight can I lose in 12 weeks?+

Most clients following the Signature Method lose 6-12kg in their first 12 weeks at a safe and sustainable rate of 0.25-0.75kg per week. Individual results depend on starting point, consistency, and adherence to the nutrition plan.
