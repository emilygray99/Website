Questions

About Elevate

How is Elevate different from 1:1 coaching?+

Elevate is a self-guided membership, you follow the programs at your own pace without personalised coaching check-ins. 1:1 coaching is fully personalised to you with weekly or fortnightly touchpoints, daily messaging, and a coach adjusting your program as you go. Elevate is the more affordable, flexible entry point.

Do I need gym access?+

No. The Elevate library includes both gym and home workout programs so you can train wherever suits you.

How do I access my programs?+

Programs are delivered through the custom Fitness By Em app. Once you join you'll get access instructions and can start immediately.

Can I cancel the Elevate Subscription at any time?+

Yes. The Elevate Subscription costs $34 per week, billed monthly. It's month-to-month with no lock-in contract. You can cancel at any time and retain access until the end of your current billing period.

Can I upgrade to 1:1 coaching later?+

Absolutely. Many women start with Elevate to build the habit of training, then step up to 1:1 coaching (the Essential Plan or Signature Method) when they're ready for more personalised support and accountability. Just book a free call and we'll figure out the right next step.

When does new content drop?+

A new program is released at the start of each month. You'll be notified when it's live. All past programs remain accessible in your library as long as you're subscribed.
