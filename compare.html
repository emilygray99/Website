Which option is right for you?

Best if:

Gym membership

You already know how to train, you are self-motivated and consistent, and you mainly need access to equipment rather than programming or guidance. Without a structured program and nutritional strategy, a gym membership alone rarely produces significant weight loss.

Best if:

Diet programs / meal delivery

You want a very short-term kickstart and understand you will need a maintenance strategy once the program ends. Research consistently shows high rates of weight regain after structured meal programs conclude because they don't build lasting behavioural skills.

Common questions

Weight loss options, answered

Is online weight loss coaching worth it?+

Online weight loss coaching is worth it for women who have struggled to lose weight independently, need accountability and personalisation, or want evidence-based guidance from a qualified professional. Research consistently shows personalised coaching and accountability are among the strongest predictors of long-term weight loss success.

What is the most effective weight loss method for women?+

The most effective weight loss method for women combines a moderate calorie deficit (300–500 calories below maintenance), high protein intake (1.6–2.2g per kg bodyweight), and progressive resistance training. Personalised coaching significantly improves adherence to all three, which is why coached weight loss programs produce better long-term outcomes than self-directed approaches.

Is a gym membership enough to lose weight?+

A gym membership alone is rarely enough for meaningful weight loss. Weight loss is primarily driven by nutrition, specifically a sustained calorie deficit. A gym provides access to equipment but not programming, accountability, or nutritional guidance. Without structure, most people don't use gym memberships consistently enough to produce significant fat loss.

Are diet programs like meal delivery services effective long term?+

Structured diet programs can produce short-term weight loss but have high relapse rates because they don't build the nutritional understanding or behavioural habits needed for long-term maintenance. When the program ends, the food choices that caused the original weight gain tend to return. Coaching that builds skills and habits produces better long-term outcomes.
