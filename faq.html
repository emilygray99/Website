Getting started
How do I know if coaching is right for me?+

If you've been trying to lose weight, build strength, or get consistent with training and haven't been able to make it stick long-term, coaching is likely the missing piece. The free 30-minute call is the best way to figure it out. No commitment required, just an honest conversation.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this too advanced for me?+

Not at all. Many of Em's most successful clients started with zero training experience. The program is built around where you are right now, not where you think you should be. You'll learn everything you need as you go, with full support throughout.

What happens on the free 30-minute call?+

It's a relaxed conversation, not a sales call. Em will ask about your goals, your current situation, any injuries or health history, and what you've tried before. From there she'll give you an honest recommendation about which program (if any) makes sense for you.

How long does it take to see results?+

Most clients start noticing changes in energy, sleep, and strength within the first 3–4 weeks. Visible body composition changes typically become apparent around weeks 6–10, depending on your starting point, consistency, and goals. That's why the minimum commitment is 12 weeks, real results take time, and that's a feature, not a bug.

Weight loss
Can I lose weight without doing extreme dieting?+

Yes, and this is actually a core part of how Em coaches. Extreme calorie restriction tends to work short term and backfire long term. The approach here focuses on sustainable calorie targets, adequate protein, progressive training, and building habits that hold up in real life. Clients lose weight and keep it off because the methods aren't punishing.

How much weight can I expect to lose?+

This depends on your starting point, consistency, and how well your body responds. A healthy, sustainable rate of fat loss is generally 0.25–0.75kg per week. Em won't promise you a number that would be dishonest, but she'll build a plan calibrated to your body and goals and adjust it as you progress. Many clients lose 6–15kg over their first 12–24 weeks.

Do I need to track calories?+

Not necessarily. If you enjoy tracking and find it useful, Em will set you up with accurate targets. If tracking feels obsessive or doesn't fit your lifestyle, she'll use alternative strategies like portion guidance and habit-based approaches that create the same calorie awareness without the mental load.

Will I have to give up foods I love?+

No. Em's nutrition approach is flexible by design, no foods are off limits, no last diet's worth of restriction rules carried over. Foods you enjoy have a place in a fat loss plan, it's about how they fit into the bigger picture. Restriction breeds obsession; flexibility breeds consistency. The goal is a way of eating you can maintain long after coaching ends.

I've tried everything and nothing has worked. Why will this be different?+

Most weight loss attempts fail because they rely on willpower over systems, or use protocols that aren't personalised to the individual. Em has a Masters Graduate in Exercise Physiology, she understands the science of how bodies respond to training and nutrition, and she builds programs around your actual body history and lifestyle. The personalisation is what makes the difference.

I'm already training regularly but not seeing results. What's different here?+

This is one of the most common messages Em gets. If you're training but the results have stalled, it's rarely about effort, it's almost always about structure. Random workouts without progressive overload, protein that's too low, or switching programs every time something new goes viral will plateau anyone. After years of coaching women, Em has found the real blocker is almost never a broken metabolism or lack of motivation, it's consistency around the basics done properly. She'll look at exactly what you're doing now and build the structure that's actually missing.

Programs & pricing
What's the difference between Essential and Signature?+

The Signature Method includes everything in Essential plus a fully personalised meal plan and weekly check-ins instead of fortnightly. If nutrition is a key part of your goal (especially for weight loss), Signature is the stronger option. If you're mainly focused on building a training habit and have nutrition reasonably sorted, Essential is a great starting point.

How long is the commitment?+

12 weeks minimum for all 1:1 coaching. This isn't arbitrary, it's the minimum time needed to build real habits, see meaningful body composition changes, and set you up for long-term success. The Elevate Subscription is month-to-month with no lock-in.

How does pricing work?+

Pricing is discussed on the free 30-minute call as it can vary based on your goals and program type. Em doesn't publish prices publicly, to ensure you're getting the right program for your situation, not just the cheapest option. The Elevate Subscription pricing is available directly on the membership page.

Training
Do I need a gym membership?+

No. Em programs for both gym and home training. If you train at home, she'll design your program around what equipment you have. If you go to a gym, she'll programme for that environment. Either way works.

How many days a week do I need to train?+

This is built around your schedule. Most clients train 3–5 days per week, but your program will reflect what's actually realistic for your life, not some ideal scenario. Consistency over a few days per week beats heroic effort followed by burnout, every time.

I've an injury or health condition. Can I still join?+

Yes. Em's background in Exercise Physiology means she's trained to work with health conditions, chronic injuries, and physical limitations. Your program will be designed around what's safe and appropriate for your body. You don't need to be in perfect health to start, in fact, proper coaching is often most valuable when things are complicated.

Nutrition
What does the meal plan include?+

The meal plan in the Signature Method is fully personalised, built around your calorie targets, food preferences, dietary restrictions, and lifestyle. It's not a generic template. Em takes into account what you actually enjoy eating and builds from there, making it far more likely you'll stick to it.

I'm vegetarian/vegan/have food allergies. Can you accommodate that?+

Absolutely. Your dietary needs and preferences are factored in from the start. Em has worked with clients across a wide range of eating styles and will build something that works for how you actually eat.

Support & coaching
How quickly does Em respond to messages?+

Em aims to respond to all messages within 24 hours on weekdays. She keeps her client roster intentionally small to make sure no one gets lost in the cracks. You're never waiting days for a response.

What if life gets in the way, travel, illness, a busy period at work?+

This is normal, and it's built into the coaching relationship. Em adjusts your program when life happens, reduced training volume during a stressful week, modified nutrition targets when you're travelling, alternative workouts when you're sick. The goal is to keep you moving forward, even when forward is slower than planned.

What happens after the 12 weeks?+

Most clients continue coaching beyond 12 weeks because the results compound over time. If you decide not to continue, you'll leave with a strong foundation, habits, knowledge, and a training approach you can sustain independently. Em also offers step-down options like the Elevate Subscription for ongoing structure without the full 1:1 commitment.
