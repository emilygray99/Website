The story

Why I do
what I do

Coaching isn't just a career for me, it came directly from my own experience trying to find something that actually worked.

I've been where you are. Overweight, uncomfortable in my own body, and stuck cycling through diets that promised everything and delivered nothing that lasted. I know what it's like to avoid photos, to dread getting dressed, to feel like your body is something you're fighting instead of living in. Going through my own weight loss journey, the slow, unglamorous, actually sustainable kind, is what led me here.

I spent years watching women, friends, clients, strangers online, bouncing between extreme diets and unsustainable training programs. Burning out, starting over, blaming themselves, when the problem was the program, not the person.

My background in exercise physiology gave me a different lens. I understood that the body is adaptive, that habits are neurological, and that sustainable results need a sustainable system, not just willpower and restriction.

So I built Fitness By Em around that principle. Every program I write is grounded in the science of how bodies respond to training. Every coaching conversation starts with the person not a template. And every 12-week commitment is designed to leave you with skills and habits that outlast the program itself.

I work exclusively with women because I've seen how often the fitness industry gets it wrong for us from programming that's just scaled-down male training to nutrition advice that ignores the menstrual cycle to coaching that prioritises aesthetics over energy and strength.

You deserve coaching built for you. That's what this is.

How I coach

What you can expect

Honesty over hype

I'll tell you what's realistic, what takes time, and what's worth doing. No empty promises, no dramatic before-and-after marketing speak.

Science not trends

Your program will be built on exercise physiology principles that have decades of research behind them, not whatever's trending on TikTok this week.

You first always

Your health history, your preferences, your life, all of it shapes the program. There's no template I'm trying to fit you into.

Consistent accountability

Check-ins, daily messaging, form checks, but more than that, I'm the person on the other side who notices when you've gone quiet, picks up the phone, and actually asks what's going on. Real support means showing up when things are going well, and when they're not.

Strength as the goal

Not just physical strength. The confidence, clarity, and self-trust that comes from learning what your body can do. That's the real result I'm after for you.

Patience with the process

12 weeks is a starting point, not a finish line. I'll teach you how to keep going long after our coaching relationship ends.

Common questions

Frequently asked

What qualifications does Emily Gray have?+

Emily Gray holds a Masters Graduate in Exercise Physiology, is an Accredited Exercise Physiologist (AEP), and is a qualified Myotherapist. She has over 5 years of online coaching experience and has helped 450+ women achieve sustainable weight loss results.

What does a Masters Graduate Exercise Physiologist do?+

A Masters Graduate Exercise Physiologist is a university-trained health professional who designs and delivers exercise programs for health, fitness, injury rehabilitation, and chronic disease management. Emily uses this clinical background, alongside her Myotherapy training, to build evidence-based weight loss programs personalised to each woman's body and lifestyle.

Does Emily only work with women?+

Yes. Emily specialises exclusively in coaching women. She has developed deep expertise in how women's bodies respond to training and nutrition, including the impact of hormones, menstrual cycle phases, and the specific physiological differences that make women's programming different from men's.

How many clients does Emily coach at once?+

Emily intentionally keeps her 1:1 client numbers small to ensure every client receives genuinely personalised attention, fast response times, and program adjustments when needed. This is part of what separates her coaching from high-volume online programs.
